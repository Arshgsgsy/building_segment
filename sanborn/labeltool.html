    Attached-part rule

    An attached part (porch, wing, den, addition, shed) is its own building when it is closed by a full-weight wall and has its own construction color or story number.

    It belongs to the main building when it is only separated by a thin line inside the main outline. Galleries and awnings are their own buildings; water tanks and cisterns are dropped. Full rule: docs/attached_part_rule.md.
