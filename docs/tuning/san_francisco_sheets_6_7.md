# San Francisco knobs on sheets 6 and 7 of the 1899 volume: unchanged (10 November 2026)

I kept the `san francisco` entry as it is (`overrides.yaml`: `{ink_contrast: 15, ink_close: 5, ink_L_dark: 35, hue_cut: 20, dash_cut: 90}`) and touched nothing in config.yaml, sanborn/, tests/, data/ or the existing outputs/overseg folders. There are no labels, so I used the label-free method of the sheet 5 tuning. Each city block (polygons in `h67.py`, drawn on `img/blocks_6.jpg` and `img/blocks_7.jpg`) runs as its own crop with 80 px of street around it. That gives six blocks on sheet 6, counting the wharf sheds, and four on sheet 7. I also reran sheet 5's four blocks. The crop runs are identical pixel for pixel to `sanborn.oversegment --crop ... --tag` runs (I checked three, `cli_check.log`, then deleted them). They reproduce the sheet 5 full-sheet numbers within a few px, so I did no full-sheet runs at all. Inside the blocks I counted uncut wall px and wall lines (straight shared-knob wall ink with the same region on both sides), regions holding two fill colors (I added a brown/olive class for the brown blocks), colored-fill coverage, small isolated fill objects and flags. The 47.1% and 39.4% CLI coverage numbers are the same artifact as on sheet 5. Inside the blocks the SF knobs cover 97.6% (sheet 6) and 94.3% (sheet 7) of the colored fill, and the rest of the "fill" is yellowed paper and stain. The current knobs are a large improvement over the shared defaults on both new sheets. They cut uncut wall lines by about 70-90% and mixed-color regions 16 → 1 and 23 → 1.

I looked at every wall line the SF knobs leave uncut (`img/s6_sf_uncut_a.png`, `_b.png`, `img/s7_sf_uncut.png`). Almost all of them are partition stubs that end inside a room, T-marks, W.N. and box symbols, letter strokes and the "No. 1" of Pier No. 1. The 9 small objects the SF knobs drop on sheet 7 (41 → 32 of 58) are all fill inside W.N. boxes, letters and stair hatching, not sheds (`img/s7_small_lost_a.png`, `_b.png`). Sheet 6 finds 2 of 7 with either knob set, and its misses are hatching and border strips. I then varied every relevant knob one at a time on top of the SF entry (`sweep1.sh`, `sweep1b.sh`, all in `results.jsonl`, `python3 table.py`). The values were ink_contrast 13/17, ink_close 4/6, ink_L_dark 30, hue_cut 0/15/25, dash_cut 0/45/60, bridge_gap 80, bridge_length 25, min_area 300 and fill_chroma 8/13. The SF values sit on a plateau. Lower contrast or a wider closing cut a few more stubs but merge 2-14 regions, lose up to 32k px of fill and add holes (`diff67.py`). Turning the hue cut off brings back 9-16 mixed regions, 15-25 give the same result, and dash_cut 45-60 changes one region. Only bridge_gap 80 is a candidate. It lowers uncut wall px on all three sheets with 0 merges on sheets 6 and 5 and 1 on sheet 7. But every wall line it newly cuts is text, a symbol or a stub ("Harness Shop", "Lodgings 2", a loft stub, W.N. boxes), its other new cuts mostly run along rows of posts and through lettering (`img/s6_bg80_splits_*.png`, `img/s7_bg80_changes.png`), and it costs a little fill. I found one real wall with a door gap that only it cuts ("3 Vac. Abv." / "Open 3d" on sheet 6 NW), and that is not enough to call it a clear gain. bridge_length 25 behaves the same, with 2-3 merges. The sheet 5 check therefore needs no new evidence: the entry is unchanged, and the sheet 5 crops give back the numbers in `outputs/tuning/san_francisco/RESULTS.md`.

On sheets 6 and 7 a labeler will still have to do the following by hand. No existing knob fixes these. Dashed interior lines are mostly not cut even with dash_cut 90. That includes the pink store rows 100-112 in sheet 6's NE block, the 320/322 Com'sn stores, the 222-228 stores in the NW block and the slanted dashed rows of the 628-640 offices on sheet 7 SE. The dashes are detected, but the lines stop short of the walls, so the cut never closes. These may be partitions in one building, but where they separate buildings the labeler cuts them. On sheet 6 SW (Commercial and East St.) one region spans Sal. & Lunch (25-27) and the D./Restt buildings (29-33). It leaks round a corner gap at the B.C. line near (1222, 6590), so a wall cut is needed there (`img/s6_sw_bottom.png`). On sheet 7 NW the solid line between Nos. 35 and 33 in the Popular Restaurant building (1750-2270, y 1630) is uncut, because its left end stops at a dashed line (`img/s7_popular_wall.png`). On the wharf the dashed-sided frame building next to the two left sheds (near 4840, 7450) is missing from the full-sheet run. Only fill_chroma 16 finds it, and that would erode pale fills everywhere, so it has to be added by hand. The Pier No. 1 and No. 3 yellow pier structures and the "Bay of San Francisco" water box come out empty, which is right for the water and the labeler's call for the piers (`img/s6_wharf_fullrun.png`, `img/s6_wharf_knobs.png`). Regions to drop: on sheet 7, the 246k px stain region inside the Palace Hotel outline (1222-1978, 6332-7023), the 23 px strip at x 5498 on the right margin, binding pieces at x < 120, the seal and pipe-symbol circles near (335, 682), (697, 887) and (2213, 3471), and the two tiny "dark" bits at (3460, 1766) and (3418, 2568). On sheet 6, the sheet number near (5410, 405), the strip at (667, 487), binding pieces at x < 60 and the scale bar at (1231, 7820). The heavy water stains along the bottom of sheet 6 make no regions in the full-sheet run. One caution for anyone reusing `h67.py`: a crop that cuts through a stain keeps the stain as a region, because crop borders count as ink support. The 460k px region around the brown block in `6_SM` and the paper region in `6_WH` are this kind of crop artifact and are absent from the full-sheet runs. My wall metric also only measures horizontal and vertical walls, so I checked the slanted walls of the triangular blocks on sheet 7 by eye (`img/s7_nw_slanted.png`, `img/s7_se_mid.png`). The SF knobs cut the solid slanted walls there, and what stays merged is separated only by dashed lines.
