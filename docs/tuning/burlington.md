# Burlington over-segmenter knobs (sheet sanborn08912_005_0005, 10 November 2026)

I propose `burlington: {min_area: 150, bridge_length: 21, min_ink_support: 0.4}` (`overrides.yaml`). I did not touch config.yaml, the code, the labels or Denver. There are no labels for this sheet, so I worked label-free, as was done for San Francisco. I drew six block outlines by hand (`img/blocks.jpg`). I tuned on B67, B69 and E1 and kept B70, B68 and E2 as the hold-out. The harness `bur.py` segments a crop around each block in-process and scores inside the outline. It measures colored-fill coverage (shared fill mask) and uncovered fill holes of 150 px or more. It checks whether each isolated fill object at least 14 px wide is found. It also counts uncut wall px: straight wall-network ink with the same region on both sides. The pale yellow turned out not to be the risk I expected. Its chroma sits at 15-35 against paper under 4, so `fill_chroma` 10 falls in a clean gap, and 8 found nothing new. The defaults already find 95 of 96 and 75 of 77 isolated fill objects, and every one they miss is a yellow bleed blob. The real losses were of three kinds. First, porch and wing pieces that a story mark ("1", "/") or an X splits into pieces under 400 px: these are dropped and leave holes, or the split rejoins them to the house across a full wall (the "1●" porch under the 2-story house at (800, 6985)). Second, partition walls that stop short at a door gap. Third, one whole building lost on the hold-out: the corrugated-iron "Ware Ho." at (1860, 6450), whose gray band is not ink, so its outline fails the 0.5 ink-support test (`img/h1_b70_warehouse.png`).

`min_area` 150 is the Uhrichsville reason again. It keeps the story-mark and X pieces and stops small pieces from being rejoined across walls. `bridge_length` 21 carries 21-30 px wall stubs across their door gaps. With 23 the remaining uncut walls were real partitions: two house-row partitions in B67 and, more serious, the Gro. store at (4740, 2530) sharing a region with the 1-story part of the house next to it (`img/t5_e1_gro.png`). The default run also had Gen'l S. and Barber at (650, 7260) in one region, and both tuned settings cut them apart (`img/h2_b70_genls.png`). `min_ink_support` 0.4 (0.45 already does it) brings back the Ware House and changes nothing else in any block. `ink_contrast` 17 or 15 also recovers it, but adds holes. `dash_cut`, `hue_cut`, `fill_chroma`, `ink_close`, `bridge_gap`, `sliver_r`, `ink_width` and `fill_smooth` gave nothing, or cost coverage (`sweep1.log`, `results.jsonl`). On the whole sheet (`fullcmp.py`), the tuned run (`outputs/overseg/sanborn08912_005_0005_burlington_tuned`) has 4 "merges" and 2 "gone" against the defaults. I looked at every one (`img/merges_final_check.png`, `img/merge_base_bl21.png`, `img/gone_b68.png`). Each merge is the loss of a false bridge cut through a porch or notch, not of a drawn wall, and both "gone" regions are bleed strips. One case is a real trade-off. In the house at (558, 3162), 23 cuts a partition between two short stubs and 21 does not. I still chose 21, because it removes more uncut walls on both halves and fixes the Gro. merge between two buildings. Its cost is cuts through numerals and the "Shed" / "Furn. Repairing" lettering, about 0.8k more uncovered fill px out of 2.6M, and more triangles in X-hatched sheds.

The labeler should expect these. There are about 75 "islands": the inside of a written "D", "2" or "o" kept as its own region, so each is a merge click. Yellow bleed blobs under the houses come as small regions to drop. X-marked sheds come in 4-8 triangles, and porches with story marks come in several pieces, so expect 2-4 regions per house. Three things still need a hand fix. The house at the top edge near (5300, 100) is not a region at all, because the code drops anything touching the sheet edge. The Ware House gets only its yellow interior, and its 15-20 px gray band is left out. The partition at (558, 3162) is still uncut. Dashed porch sides are never cut. That is fine under the attached-part rule, but the porch fill spills past the dashes until dissolve fits the outlines. The other remaining "uncut walls" I checked (`img/uncut_hold.png`) are bleed strips along outer walls and the "2=3½" line at St. John's Hall, not real walls.
