# Sacramento over-segmenter knobs (sheet sanborn00799_001_0010L, 10 November 2026)

I propose `sacramento: {ink_contrast: 15, ink_close: 5, hue_cut: 20, dash_cut: 90, min_area: 250}` (`overrides.yaml`). There are no labels, so I tuned label-free like San Francisco. I drew six block outlines (`img/blocks.jpg`) and ran `segment` in-process on one crop per block (`sac.py`), with every crop border treated as a sheet edge. That last part matters on this sheet. The yellowed and stained paper has chroma 10-37, as high as the pink fill (17-25), so the street paper is "fill" and forms one huge component that reaches the sheet edge. Every building connected to it through a dashed side, a door gap or a faint wall is dropped together with it, and so is everything touching the right edge, where the spread is cut. With the borders treated as edges, my crop results match the full-sheet run pixel for pixel inside every block, for both the default and the tuned knobs. The CLI's 36.7% fill coverage is mostly this paper. Inside the blocks the defaults cover 85% of the fill, but they dropped about half of the NE block's lower half (W.P. Fuller, Sash & Door, Lodgings, all connected to the cut edge building) and left 92 uncut wall lines and 19 regions holding two fill colors. `fill_chroma` cannot separate the stains from the pink (13 and 16 change little, and 20 drops pink), and `min_ink_support` changes nothing. So the fix is closing the walls. `ink_contrast` 15 with `ink_close` 5 does it: NEb coverage goes 51% → 84%, and uncut wall px drop by about 75%. Each knob alone gets only part of that. `hue_cut` 20 takes mixed-color regions to 0 (20, 30, 40 and 50 all give 0). `dash_cut` 90 cuts dashed partitions such as the Drugs / S. stores at 1031-1029. `min_area` 250 brings back small yellow boxes (SE small fill objects 3/5 → 5/5) and adds cuts without merges. I left out `ink_L_dark` 35 because it made no difference here. I rejected `ink_close` 6-7 (it loses whole small regions), `bridge_length` 25 (it cuts through letters and skylight boxes), `ink_contrast` 13 (it loses small objects) and `min_ink_cc` 30-150 (it does not close the dashed outlines, and with the ink knobs it costs small objects).

I chose the knobs on NW, SE and NEb (the lower half of NE, the one block where the edge drop was fixable). Only then did I score SW, NEt and the sliver block E5 at the right edge of the bottom half. The hold-out confirms the wall result: uncut wall px fall 82%, the 12 mixed-color regions go to 0, and `diff.py` finds two merges. On inspection both are losses of false cuts, not missed walls: a bridge through a "7" box in SW, and the X diagonals of the "(Old)" Barn Bst building in NEt (`img/h_net_merge.png`). SW coverage falls 94.7% → 92.6%. I looked at the lost pixels (`lost.py`, `img/h_lost_blobs.png`): they are holes among text and hatching and strips along walls the defaults had left uncut, which dissolve's pocket and crack fill should close. The one real loss is a single tiny yellow shed at 1604,5524 in the SW crop, which the full-sheet run still keeps (`img/labeler_warnings.jpg`). On the full sheet, suspects go 48 → 42, dark flags 4 → 1, regions outside the blocks 23 → 34 (mostly a few stain specks and the yellow strip to the right of SW, which is real), and the stain-only street crops keep 8 regions (the stained letters of "SECOND" and "(80 feet wide)").

A labeler will still have to fix a lot by hand on this sheet. The fixes need code (fill measured against the local paper color, or no edge drop on a cut spread), not knobs. Everything touching the right sheet edge is dropped: Adams Bldg and the yellow front strip in NE, and almost all of the E5 block in the bottom right (only 6% covered). The yellow frame strips around the blocks have a dashed outer side, so they leak into the stained paper and vanish: the whole perimeter of the Western Hotel block, including the yellow 1019 Rest't at its top right corner; the "Shed (old) on posts" column, the left strip and the 1130-1124 strip of NW's lower half; and the top-left corner and the 1115-1103 awning strip of SW. These need ignore boxes or hand-drawn buildings. Tiny yellow "1X" sheds of about 30 px in the SW yards are mostly missed with either knob set. The cistern circle at 3855,7022 now comes in two pieces and has lost its "round" flag, so drop it by hand. Text-heavy and stained pink buildings, such as the Western Hotel rooms and the Sacramento Cracker Co., come in many ragged pieces to merge. Rows split only by dashed lines are cut now, and some of those may be partitions of one building.
