# Savannah over-segmenter knobs (sheet sanborn01500_003_0012, 10 November 2026)

I propose `savannah: {ink_close: 5, min_ink_support: 0.4, bridge_gap: 80, hue_cut: 20, dash_cut: 90, min_area: 200}` (`overrides.yaml`). I did not edit config.yaml, the code, tests or any label folder, and I never ran a Denver sheet. There are no labels, so I followed the San Francisco method on crops. I drew ten block outlines by hand (`img/blocks.jpg`) and ran `segment` in-process on one padded crop per block, with the paper color taken from the whole sheet as the CLI's `--crop` does (`sv.py`). I scored uncut straight wall-network ink, mixed pink/yellow/gray regions, isolated small fill objects, and fill coverage inside the blocks. I tuned on five crops: the Southern Lamp Black Works inset, the S.P. Shotter resin plant, and blocks 42, 59 and 76. I held out blocks 60, 77, 81 and 82 and the Vale Royal Dairy. The biggest problem with the defaults was recall, not walls. The two large yellow Collecting Chambers in the lamp-black inset (about 480k px, a third of all colored fill in the industrial part) were dropped completely, which is why the CLI coverage was only 85.8%. Their fill is ringed by a 20 px gray band (L* about 75), and the black outline sits outside that band, more than `grow` px from the fill. So only 40-50% of the outline counts as ink. `min_ink_support` 0.4 keeps both chambers (lamp coverage 13% → 98.5%, `img/final_lamp.png`). On every other crop it changes nothing, held-out ones included, and 0.45 is not enough. `ink_contrast` 15, `ink_width` 25 and `min_ink_cc` 150 also bring the chambers back, but they rework the ink everywhere, and on block 76 `ink_contrast` 15 even leaves more walls uncut.

For walls, `ink_close` 5 helped most. It closes the small gaps where partition walls meet the outer wall, so for example 561/559 on Olive St. is now cut. It also removes false bridge cuts through the "1 D." lettering, which shows up as 4 apparent merges that are really fixes (`img/close5_merges.png`, `img/close5_splits.png`). Uncut wall px on the tune blocks went 941 → 598 with it. `bridge_gap` 80 only adds cuts. It cuts the dashed party wall of the pink 565/563 double house and separates the small sheds below the Cooper Shop (`img/A_bg80_hue.png`). `dash_cut` 90 cuts the pink 575-73-71 double house along its dashed line. `hue_cut` 20 cuts the pink box out of the dashed yellow shed at Shotter and fixes the two regions in the sheet that mix pink and yellow. `min_area` 200 keeps three or four tiny porch and step boxes under 400 px. It also lets bridge cuts leave small pieces, for example thin rear strips across a wall in block 81. I checked each knob by dropping it from the set on all ten crops, and every one is needed: uncut px go up by 290 without the hue cut, 155 without the dash cut, 1,160 without bridge_gap and 830 without ink_close. `bridge_length` 27/25 cut mostly through letters (`img/B_bl27.png`), `ink_close` 6 loses small objects, and `fill_chroma` 13 drops two small stills, so I left those shared. The numbers are below (blocks only; "tune" leaves out the lamp inset, whose kept chambers add 979 px of their own inner double line). I chose the knobs on the tune crops and only then ran the held-out blocks. They show the same picture: uncut walls -46% (19 → 10 lines of 30 px or more), 0 mixed regions, no structure lost, and 3 merges. I looked at all 3, and 2 are lost false cuts while 1 is an ambiguous opening (`img/held_merges_gone.png`). The 10 uncut lines left are partition stubs that end inside a building, X diagonals and a dashed stub in the colored school (`img/held_uncut.png`). The full-sheet run `outputs/overseg/sanborn01500_003_0012_savannah_tuned` gives 761 → 871 regions, coverage 85.8% → 90.6%, suspects 16 → 10, round flags 4 → 4 and dark flags 0 → 2.

The worst case is the Shotter Storage chain (`img/remaining_storage_chain.png`): the big Storage, the tank room, the long Storage, the small Storage and the Cooper Shop are still one region. They are drawn rotated about 20°, with slanted dashed partitions, and neither the bridge nor the dashed-line cut works off the horizontal and vertical, so these need four hand cuts. Off. and Supplies are already separate. The Varnish Rm / Wood Working building at Alfred Kent's has an L-shaped dashed partition that also stays whole. The Collecting Chambers come as two regions. The band between their double middle line goes to the upper chamber, and the gray border bands and the gray "Iron Clad" strips beside the furnaces are thin separate regions or not covered. The gray oil tanks with corrugated covers at Shotter are not found (no chroma), so add them or give them ignore boxes if they count as structures. The cost of the change is junk, almost all of it from `min_area` 200 and easy to drop. Off the blocks there are about 23 more small regions (46 against 23): paper blobs between the dashed water-pipe lines along Pine St., pieces of the scale bar, two letters of the Library of Congress stamp, a strip in the left margin, and two "dark" flags at the bottom sheet edge (`img/full_outside_new.png`). Inside the blocks there are a few more X-hatch triangle pieces, stair-hatching strips and thin stain strips along walls. Holes of 150 px or more go up slightly on the held-out blocks (120 → 129), where `ink_close` 5 joins lettering into ink. Dissolve's pocket fill should close them.
