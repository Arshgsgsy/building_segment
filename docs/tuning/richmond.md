# Richmond over-segmenter knobs (sheet sanborn09064_005_0003, 10 November 2026)

I propose `richmond: {hue_cut: 20, hue_chroma: 10, dash_cut: 90, bridge_length: 25, min_area: 250}` (`overrides.yaml`). All five only add cuts or regions. I changed nothing in `config.yaml`, `sanborn/`, tests, labels or the manifest. The sheet has no labels, so I judged it the San Francisco way (`rich.py`). I measured the nine block outlines by ink projection (`img/blocks.jpg`) and ran the real `segment` on each block plus 80 px of street. These crop runs reproduce the full-sheet default run region for region. I tuned on rows 1-2 (blocks 712/711/710/812/811/810, "T") and only looked at row 3 (912/911/910, "H") after choosing. The defaults were already good on solid walls and fill. The low CLI fill coverage (87.1%) is a metric artifact, as it was on SF: inside the blocks the defaults cover 98.3% (T) and 97.2% (H) of the colored fill. The CLI number is pulled down by about 0.8M px of tinted paper outside the blocks, of which only 8% is in a region. The real problems were these. First, yellow frame sheds, porches and iron-clad strips drawn against pink brick with only a dashed line or no line were not cut apart. That left 7 mixed-color regions on T (`img/mixed_base.png`, `img/uncut_base.png`, e.g. the two yellow sheds inside the Bainbridge St. Baptist Church). Second, dashed partitions and porch lines were never cut. Third, wall stubs ending in a door gap were not cut. Fourth, a few dashed porches and porch wedges were lost.

`hue_cut` 20 does the most work. It needs `hue_chroma` 10, because Richmond's pink is very pale (C* about 13-14, hue about -20°; yellow is C* 38 at 90°, blue C* 24 at -110°). At the shared 15 the pink has no hue, and the cut leaves 6 of the 7 small mixed regions. Chroma 8, 10 and 12 give identical results, and hue_cut 20 to 40 give the same splits. All 10 hue splits on T follow a real color boundary (`img/hue_splits_T.png`): church sheds, the four IRON CLAD strips on Hull St., PORCH 2°, a T.CL. box and the slated spire. `dash_cut` 90 cuts dashed store partitions and porch-from-house lines (`img/dc90_splits_T.png`). It also chops the paper spill beyond dashed porch sides into small extra pieces. 45 added only more of those pieces. `bridge_length` 25 cuts door-gap partitions and porches. 23 and 21 cut through letters and octagon symbols, and 21 cut a house diagonally (`img/bl21_splits_a.png`, `img/bl23_splits_b.png`). `min_area` 250 brings back dashed-porch pieces and porch corners cut off by a "1"/"2", including the porch at (1088, 3315) that the defaults dropped (`img/ma250_new_T.png`). Its cost is 3-4 junk regions on T, letter loops of "D." and "O". 200 only added slivers along walls (`img/ma200_new_T.png`). I rejected `ink_close` 5 and `ink_contrast` 17/15: they merged regions and dropped some. I also rejected `bridge_gap` 80: about half its 13 cuts extend outline steps into a house, and it gained only 3% on walls. `fill_chroma`, `min_ink_support`, the `dash_*` keep rules, `ink_L_dark`, `ink_width` and `min_ink_cc` had no effect or were worse.

The hold-out confirms the choice. On row 3, uncut wall px fall 74%, and the one remaining wall line is a text stroke in the Christadelphian Church label. Mixed regions go to 0. Nothing merges, and no small object is lost. I looked at all 20 hold-out splits (`img/hold_splits_a.png`, `img/hold_splits_b.png`). They are room partitions of the big yellow houses, the rear "2" wing of a house, IR.CL. and AUTO strips, door-gap sheds and the partial walls of the "16 22 16" building. The false cuts are the "1" strokes of two tiny "1o" sheds, each now in two pieces, and the X-hatch triangles, which are cut along the X by design. The full-sheet run is `outputs/overseg/sanborn09064_005_0003_richmond_tuned`.

The cost is mostly fill, and some of it labelers will have to fix by hand. Coverage drops by about 0.25 points: thin strips of the second color (under about 20 px) become slivers and are dropped. Examples are the pink strip under the yellow shed at (2951, 1959), a pink band at (2831, 5493) and the tan diagonal band in the "Office" at (3843, 5237) (`img/lost_T.png`, `img/lost_H.png`). Those need a manual add, or rely on dissolve. Labelers will also see more small junk that they need to drop or merge. That means paper spill pieces beyond dashed porch sides, from `dash_cut`, and letter loops inside "D."/"O", from `min_area` 250. A few walls are still missed and need hand cuts. They are partitions that end in an open room, such as (2012, 3615), (4743, 763) and (2735, 5925). The "WALL 1st ONLY" partial walls in the City Offices/Bank and the store at (1724, 4412) also need hand cuts if they should be separate parts, as do slanted or broken dashed lines. Some dashed yellow porches under a building are still not found, such as the porch below the blue building at (3178, 7129). Outside stairs and ladder-hatched porches, such as (4948, 2122) and (4609, 3395), have no region either. The small blue (fireproof) buildings in block 911 come out as their own regions, and the outlined X-hatched sheds come in triangles that need merging. The two "dark" flags and the "round" flag are unchanged from the defaults and lie outside the blocks. The harness, every run (`results.jsonl`, `sweep1-3.sh`, `hold1.sh`) and the T/H label maps (`runs/`) are in this folder.
