# Salt Lake City over-segmenter knobs (sheet sanborn08891_003_0008, 10 November 2026)

I propose `salt lake city: {ink_close: 5, ink_contrast: 17, min_ink_cc: 200, fill_chroma: 8, min_area: 250, hue_cut: 20}` (`overrides.yaml`). Every one of these only adds ink cuts, fill or regions. There are no labels, so I tuned label-free the way San Francisco was done. I drew the block outline by hand along its lot lines (`slc.py`, `BLOCK`, checked on `img/sheet_grid.jpg`). Then I ran the segmenter in-process on seven tune crops from the west, north and the olive buildings on the east side, and kept five held-out crops for the east-middle, the south-east and south rows, and the north-centre rows. The checks were the same as San Francisco's: uncut straight wall-network ink (shared-knob ink, the same region on both sides), regions mixing two fill classes (pink, yellow, olive/gray, green, blue), coverage of the colored fill and holes of 150 px or more, small isolated fill objects, and a look by eye at every flagged case. Most of the gain comes from `ink_close` 5. The walls on this sheet are drawn broken by crosses, numbers and wall notes, and the closing joins them, so a wall cuts and bridges where it used to stop short. That one knob halves the uncut wall px on the tune crops, and it also cuts the Billiard Rm off its Passage strip and splits the Saddle Shop and G.A.R. Hall group (`img/t2_billiard_passage.png`). `ink_contrast` 17 cuts the faint pink/yellow, pink/blue and olive/yellow seams (mixed regions on the tune crops go 5 to 2, `img/t_mixed_olive_yellow.png`). 15 adds only lettering strokes and more holes. `min_ink_cc` 200 keeps the 40-px wall pieces between the words of notes such as "WALL TO ROOF", so the party wall between the two stacked 3B buildings in the Eagle Block finally cuts (`img/t1_wall_to_roof*.png`). With the default 300, those pieces counted as text and left a 93 px gap that no bridge spans. `fill_chroma` 8 is for the gray/olive (adobe or stone) fill. Its chroma is only 11-15 against 10 for the defaults, while the gray paper sits at C* 0-2. So the olive buildings were found, but they came out speckled and ragged. At 8 they are solid (`img/t3_olive_fill.png`). `min_area` 250 brings back small fill pieces that the closing fragments, and `hue_cut` 20 splits pink brick bands and pink parts from yellow, green and olive fills.

I chose the values on the tune crops only. There, uncut wall px go 7,202 to 1,554, wall lines 20 to 3, mixed regions 5 to 2, and small objects found 96 to 103 of 115, while coverage goes 97.9% to 97.0%. On the held-out crops, which I scored only after choosing, the same values give 12,210 to 3,870 uncut wall px (-68%), 36 to 8 wall lines, 12 to 1 mixed regions (the hue cut takes them from 8 to 1, `img/h_mixed_I_vs_hue20.png`), small objects found 57 to 65 of 82, and coverage 96.4% to 95.0%. So the result carries over. `diff.py` reports one "merge" against the defaults on each crop set. Both are the loss of a false bridge cut across a single room (the Bake House box and the "Rooms & Off. Ab'v." room, `img/t2_bakehouse_false_cut.png`, `img/h1_apparent_merge.png`), not a wall. The "gone" regions are slivers between stair hatching and text that the closing turns into ink. The drop in the CLI fill coverage is not a loss. `fill_chroma` 8 adds 1.8M fill px, of which only 0.36M lie in the block; the rest is the gray margins and the border. The extra dark flags are 7 bits of the bottom-left sheet margin (y about 7720-7760). I tried and left out `ink_contrast` 15 and 16, `ink_close` 4 and 6, `bridge_length` 25 (+250 regions, mostly cuts through text), `bridge_gap` 80, `ink_width` 21, `dash_cut` 45/90 (almost nothing here), `fill_chroma` 7, `min_area` 200/300 and `ink_L_dark` 35 (`sweep1-4.log`, `held.log`, `results.jsonl`).

The cost is about 2.5 times as many regions and more holes. Text-heavy fills (the Natatorium lettering, Undertaker Shop, the "IR.CL. Roof 8' High" box) come in many pieces. Labelers will have to merge a lot, and merging back the pink brick bands that the hue cut splits off yellow, green and olive fills along the walls. They will still have to cut a few things by hand. Olive adobe dwellings ("D.") joined to a yellow shed or strip by only a dashed line stay merged, because yellow and olive are only about 8° apart in hue. Examples are the west yard near 1330-1570,4190-4300 and 1480-1670,4510-4700, and the olive building with its yellow "2" strip at 4490-4700,2350-2490. The L-shaped line inside the olive building at 3391,5648 and the wall stubs in the yellow Carriage Shed (2471,3105) also stay uncut. So do the dashed partitions of the Cliff House row along Third South, which may be one building. They should drop the junk on the sheet margins and on the E. Temple address strip (x about 5125-5145) and check the X-hatched yellow boxes, such as 3156,3427, which come out fragmented and partly unlabeled. Stair interiors are also left unlabeled, and dissolve's pocket fill closes most of them. The scripts are `slc.py` (harness), `show.py`, `boxes.py`, `mixed.py`, `diff.py`, `listbox.py` and `fullcheck.py` (whole-block check of the CLI runs, `fullcheck.log`/`.json`), with evidence crops in `img/`.
